# Fatigue and rest
Fatigue can impair attention and driving performance. Adequate sleep before driving is essential. If a driver becomes sleepy, they should stop at a safe location and rest. A monitoring score cannot establish that someone is fit to drive. This paragraph summarizes general NHTSA guidance; it is not individualized advice.

# Breaks and planning
Plan rest opportunities before a long journey and begin well rested. A brief alert from a monitoring device does not replace sleep or remove fatigue. Stop safely when sleepy rather than attempting to continue a trip using alerts alone.
